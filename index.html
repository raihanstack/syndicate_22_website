Who We Are

About Syndicate 22

We Are Like Family

Syndicate 22 is a football team made by old students of Chhagalnaiya Academy, mostly from the SSC 2022 batch. We are friends who love football and always support each other.

We play matches, have fun, and make great memories together — both on the field and outside. Syndicate 22 is all about friendship and teamwork.

Our Goal

We want to play football, have fun with friends, and keep our school memories alive.

Our Vision

We dream of making Syndicate 22 a well-known team that makes our school and batch proud.
