About Syndicate 22

Our Team, Our Brotherhood

Syndicate 22 is a football team formed by the ex-students of Chhagalnaiya Academy, mainly from the SSC 2022 batch. Built on friendship, unity, passion, and a deep love for football, we stand together as one strong family.

We play together, grow together, and create unforgettable memories on and off the pitch. Syndicate 22 represents the spirit of brotherhood, discipline, and the bond of our batch.

Our Goal

To enjoy football together, stay connected as friends, and keep our batch memories alive.

Our Vision

To make Syndicate 22 a legendary and united football team that represents our school and batch with pride.
